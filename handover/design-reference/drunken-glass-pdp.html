Good to know

Questions, answered

What if it arrives broken?
We replace it free. Film one continuous unboxing video, and if a piece arrived broken, send it to us within 48 hours of delivery — a replacement ships out, with nothing to send back. Replacement only; we don't offer refunds, and intact orders can't be returned for a change of mind.
How long does delivery take?
Orders dispatch in 2–3 working days, with free shipping across India. Designs that are out of stock can be preordered and arrive in 15–20 working days.
Can I mix designs and colours in one order?
Yes — use "Build a set" above to mix any designs in any quantity. The volume discount applies to your total count, and sets arrive in a mix of colours and designs.
Is it dishwasher safe?
Hand-cut crystal is best hand-washed, to keep the cut edges sharp and the glass clear. Our borosilicate range — tumblers and coffee glasses — is fully dishwasher-proof.
