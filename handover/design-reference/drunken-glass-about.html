Chapter one

It began with heat.

Fifteen years ago, two brothers stood in front of a furnace and decided to learn the hardest glass there is.

Borosilicate — the glass that takes boiling and freezing without a flinch, the kind labs and kitchens stake their work on. It is unforgiving to make and honest to hold. We got good at it. Good enough that the world started buying.

Our glass went out by the crate — to restaurants, hotels and homes in countries we'll likely never visit. People raised it at weddings and poured into it on ordinary Tuesdays. It held their water, their wine, their mornings.

And every single piece left our floor wearing someone else's name.

For fifteen years, our best work wore a stranger's label.

Amit & Rahul Gupta

Chapter two

Why we finally signed our name.

There's a specific ache in making something people love and staying invisible behind it. For a long time we told ourselves the work was enough. One day it wasn't.

We didn't want to keep pouring our craft into other people's brands. We wanted our own name on the base of the glass — and we wanted to bring it home first, to our own country, to the people closest to us.

So we lit the same furnace for ourselves. We drew a fill line on every glass — the honest level it's meant to hold. And we sell it the way it should be sold: straight from our floor to your table, with no distributor, no markup, no one standing in between.

Drunken Glass isn't a new company pretending to be a maker. It's fifteen years of making, finally under our own name.
