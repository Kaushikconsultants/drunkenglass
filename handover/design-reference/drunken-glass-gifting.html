Gifting FAQ

How does the sample process work?
Once we agree the design and branding, we produce one branded sample for you to approve. Nothing goes into full production until you've signed off — and the 15–20 working day lead time starts from that approval, not from your enquiry.
What artwork do you need for engraving?
A vector file of your logo (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) gives the cleanest engraving. If you only have a PNG or JPEG, send it anyway — we can usually redraw it, and we'll flag it if the detail is too fine to cut well at glass scale.
Can different recipients get different glasses?
Yes. We can split a run across designs and across addresses — for example, a decanter for senior clients and tumblers for the wider team, shipped to separate locations from one order.
What's the realistic deadline for a festive order?
Work back at least six weeks from the date you need gifts in hand: roughly a week for quote and sample approval, 15–20 working days for branded production, then delivery. For Diwali, that means starting in early autumn. Tell us your date on the form and we'll tell you honestly if it's achievable.
Do we get a GST invoice for the order?
Yes — every corporate order is invoiced with GST against your company details, so it books cleanly as a business expense. Corporate orders are 100% prepaid, and we raise a proforma invoice your accounts team can process.
