From the furnace

We don't buy glass.
We make it.

For fifteen years our furnace has drawn borosilicate — the glass with so little thermal expansion it goes freezer-to-oven without a crack and shrugs off a thousand dishwasher cycles. The glass labs and kitchens trust.

That same mastery is now hand-cut into our crystal line, and everything ships straight off our floor. No distributor stands between our furnace and your table.

For a home, direct means a fair price and glass we stand behind. For a restaurant, it means real capacity — the same hundred glasses, run after run.

The two brothers

The world drank from our glass.
It never knew our name.

For fifteen years, two brothers — Amit and Rahul Gupta — have shaped glass at one furnace, and shipped it to tables the world over, to people who never learned who made it. Every cut was ours. The label was always someone else's.

Drunken Glass is us, at last, with our name on the glass — selling to our own country, direct from our own floor. No middleman. No borrowed label. Just the makers, and you.
