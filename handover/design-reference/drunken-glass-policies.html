Shipping policy

We ship across India, free, on every order.

Charges

Shipping is free on all orders within India. There are no minimum-order requirements to qualify.

Dispatch & delivery times

  • In-stock items: dispatched within 2–3 working days of order confirmation.
  • Delivery: typically [3–7] working days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • Preorder / made-to-order items: 15–20 working days.
  • Branded corporate gifting orders: 15–20 working days after sample approval.

Working days exclude Sundays and public holidays. Remote PIN codes may take longer.

Tracking

You'll receive tracking details by email and SMS once your order is dispatched. You can also track it any time on our Track Order page.

Packaging

Glass is packed for transit in protective inserts. If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, please film the unboxing before opening further — see the Returns & Replacement policy below.

Payment methods

We ship across all of India. We accept UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets and Cash on Delivery for retail orders. Paying online gets you 5% off and avoids COD handling charges.

Undelivered & refused parcels

If a COD parcel is refused or repeatedly undeliverable, we may restrict COD on future orders from that address. Any prepaid amount on a returned-to-origin parcel is refunded after we receive the goods back, less any actual courier cost incurred.

Returns & replacement policy

Glass travels a long way. If it doesn't survive the trip, that's on us.

✦ Anything that arrives broken is replaced free. We ask for an unboxing video — it's what lets us replace it at no cost, without an investigation.

What's covered

  • Items that arrive broken, cracked or chipped in transit.
  • Items that arrive manufacturing-defective.
  • The wrong item or wrong quantity delivered.

How to claim

  1. Film the unboxing. One continuous video, from the sealed parcel to the glass in your hand.
  2. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@drunkenglass.in with your order ID and the video.
  3. We ship a replacement. Nothing to pack up, nothing to send back.

Without the unboxing video we can't distinguish transit breakage from later accidental damage, so we may be unable to approve a free replacement.

Resolution

Our standard resolution is a free replacement of the affected item. Where a replacement isn't possible — for example a design is discontinued — we will offer a suitable alternative or a refund of that item.

What isn't covered

  • Change of mind on correctly delivered, intact goods.
  • Damage from use, washing, dropping or thermal shock after delivery.
  • Claims raised after the 48-hour window without an unboxing video.
  • Minor variation in colour, cut or size — every piece is hand-cut, and small differences are a feature of handmade glass, not a defect.

Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 in respect of goods that are defective, deficient or not as described.

Refunds, where applicable

Where a refund is due, it is processed to the original payment method within [7–10] working days of approval. COD orders are refunded by bank transfer to details you provide.

Order cancellation

You can cancel an order any time before dispatch by contacting us — we'll refund in full. Once dispatched, the replacement policy above applies. Custom and branded gifting orders cannot be cancelled once production has begun.
