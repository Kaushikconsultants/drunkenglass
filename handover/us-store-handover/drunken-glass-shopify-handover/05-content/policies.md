# Policies (plain-language drafts)

**Status: drafts for legal review.** Paste into Shopify > Settings > Policies after review. The help page and footer link to each one.

## shipping
- orders ship within 1 to 2 business days.
- free US shipping on orders over $75. below that, a flat $6.95.
- delivery estimates are shown on product pages, at checkout and in your confirmation email.
- we currently ship within the US.

## returns and breakage
- arrived broken? email us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we’ll send a free replacement.
- changed your mind? unused glasses in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days.
- refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days of the return reaching us.

## privacy
- we collect only what we need to fulfill your order and support you: name, email, phone and address.
- we don’t sell your personal information. california residents can request access to or deletion of their data and opt out of sharing for targeted ads.
- payment details are handled by our payment partners, not stored by us.

## terms
- prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax, which is calculated at checkout.
- digital gift cards can be used on anything in the store.
- colors may vary slightly from screen to glass.
- one discount code per order unless stated.

## accessibility
- we’re working to meet WCAG 2.1 AA across the store.
- having trouble using the site? email us and we’ll help you complete your order.
