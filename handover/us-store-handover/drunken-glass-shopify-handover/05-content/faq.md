# FAQ (help page)

## how long does shipping take?
orders ship within 1 to 2 business days. every product page shows a delivery estimate for your zip code, and tracking lands in your inbox the moment it ships.

## where are your glasses made?
in our own borosilicate furnace in india, where we’ve made glassware for about 15 years. designed by us, made by us, shipped to you.

## how much is shipping?
free on US orders over $75. below that it’s a flat $6.95.

## what if my glass arrives broken?
email us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we’ll send a free replacement. no forms, no fuss.

## can i pour hot coffee or tea?
yes. borosilicate handles sudden temperature changes much better than regular glass, so hot coffee and iced drinks are both fine. keep it away from direct flame and stovetops.

## is it dishwasher and microwave safe?
every product page shows whether that design is dishwasher and microwave safe. printed and colored designs keep their colors longest with a gentle hand wash.

## do you sell gift cards?
yes. digital gift cards from $25 to $150, delivered by email right away or on a date you pick.

## what’s your return policy?
unused glasses in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## how can i pay?
all major cards, apple pay, google pay, shop pay, paypal and pay-in-4 at checkout.

## do you do bulk or corporate gifting?
yes, from 25 pieces. custom colors, logo printing and gift boxes are possible. use the form on the gifting page.
